1. Project Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard that turns raw employee data into visual insight. It helps HR teams see who is leaving, from where, and why by comparing attrition across departments, salary ranges, job roles, age groups, gender and experience.

4. Key Insights

Attrition is 19.4% — about 1 in 5 employees

6 of 31 employees left. This is a moderate-to-high level for a single team and worth monitoring.

Higher pay bands lose more people

The 10+ LPA band has the highest rate (3 of 6 = 50%), then 6–10 LPA (2 of 8 = 25%). 3–6 LPA lost nobody. Pay alone is not keeping senior or well-paid staff.

Satisfied employees still left

Of the 6 Sales Executive leavers, 3 rated satisfaction 4 (high) and 2 rated 1 (low). Satisfaction scores alone do not predict exits, so career growth, workload or management should be explored.

Exits are concentrated in Sales Executives and Marketing

All attrition sits in the Sales Executive role within Marketing; Sales Representatives show none.

Males account for most exits

5 of 6 leavers were male. Gender totals are needed to confirm whether the rate is actually higher.

5. Business Recommendations

Review pay and growth for 6+ LPA roles

Introduce career paths, promotions and retention bonuses for higher-paid Sales Executives.

Run stay and exit interviews

Because satisfaction scores did not explain the exits, collect direct feedback on manager support, targets and growth.

Target the 5–15 year experience window

Offer mentoring and role changes to mid-career staff where exits cluster.

Track metrics monthly

Refresh the dashboard monthly and set an alert if attrition passes an agreed threshold, such as 15%.

6. Limitations & Future Scope

Limitations

  • Small sample (31 records), so percentages swing easily.
  • Only one department and one age group in view.
  • No exit-reason or performance data.

Future scope

  • Add all departments and more records.
  • Predict attrition risk with machine learning.
  • Add tenure, promotion and manager data.
  • Publish to Power BI Service with live refresh.

7. Conclusion

The HR Analytics Dashboard gives HR teams a clear, filterable view of attrition. In this dataset, 19.4% of employees left, mostly Sales Executives in Marketing, with higher-paid staff leaving at the highest rate. These findings support targeted retention actions and better workforce planning.
